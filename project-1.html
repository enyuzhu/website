Info

Hostelworld is the leading online travel booking platform specialized in hostels and budget-friendly accommodations around the world and connecting solo travelers. The previous Meet Travelers Version 2 feature helps recommends travelers to users based on standard cosine similarity search on background information. I expanded the recommendation system by incorporating image embeddings and personality-based signals, capturing behavioral characteristics such as extraversion alongside visual preferences and aesthetics. This work explored how visual and personality signals can better understand traveler compatibility to surface more relevant social connections and improved Hit@5 for direct messages by 53%.

See more

Challenge

Meet Travelers Version 2 recommended travelers using standard cosine similarity search on background information alone. This approach missed the visual and personality-based signals that shape real compatibility between travelers.

Approach

I expanded the recommendation system by incorporating image embeddings alongside personality-based signals, capturing behavioral characteristics like extraversion in addition to visual preferences and aesthetics. The model was trained on approximately 600K direct messages and evaluated on a held-out set of approximately 150K messages.

Solution

The result is a hybrid recommendation architecture that blends visual and personality signals to better model traveler compatibility, surfacing more relevant social connections between travelers.

Outcome

Improved Hit@5 for direct messages by 53%.
