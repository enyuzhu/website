About

I'm Enyu, pleasure to e-meet you!

I'm double-majoring in artificial intelligence and architecture at MIT. My work lies my work at the intersection of technology, design, and social experiences.

Growing up in cities like Guangzhou and NYC, I was captivated by how the built environment shapes human experience. That fascination led me to pursue architecture at design firms such as SOM, HOK, and FxCollaborative. With growing curiosity and love for computer science, I began to bridge design, social experience, and machine learning through personal projects and roles at the MIT Media Lab, CSAIL, NUS Urban Analytics Lab, and Hostelworld.

I care about building personal, accessible, and emotional experiences for a more social and creative world through technology. Let's make the world a better place together!
